Radbank, Fullcurebank and Pathbank by Lexmedix

Radbank writes radiology reports from dictation, Fullcurebank writes consultations and discharges, Pathbank reads lab reports. See what each does.

By Dr Sudhanshu Bankata · 13 October 2026 · 3 min read

On this page

  1. Radbank: dictate only what is abnormal
  2. Fullcurebank: consultations, ward rounds and discharges
  3. Pathbank: a clear reading of a lab report
  4. What they cost
  5. Next step

Radbank, Fullcurebank and Pathbank are three of the six AI apps our team builds at Lexmedix. Radbank by Lexmedix writes radiology and other diagnostic reports from what a specialist dictates, Fullcurebank by Lexmedix writes consultations, ward rounds and discharges as the doctor works, and Pathbank by Lexmedix reads a lab report and shows the flags and the trend.

In all three, the AI drafts and organises. The doctor still reviews and decides.

Radbank: dictate only what is abnormal

Radbank is the reporting tool for radiologists and other diagnostic specialists. Structured CT, MRI, ultrasound and X-ray templates open with normal findings in every section, so a radiologist dictates only what is not normal.

  • You can say all findings in one go. Radbank puts each one in the right section and changes only those sections.
  • Automatic RADS scoring (BI-RADS, LI-RADS, Lung-RADS, TI-RADS, PI-RADS and O-RADS) is added to the impression.
  • A seven-module quality check reviews completeness, language, clinical logic, differentials, guideline compliance, recommendations and risk flags.
  • An AI second opinion gives a structured second read of the report in one click.

Details are on the Radbank page.

Fullcurebank: consultations, ward rounds and discharges

Fullcurebank is built mainly for doctors. In Conversation OPD you talk with the patient as usual, and Fullcurebank listens and builds the consultation note: complaint, history, examination, medicines and plan. It never fills the note on its own. You review a sheet and tick what to apply.

  • Quick OPD and SOAP OPD are there for doctors who prefer to dictate or type the encounter.
  • Differentials, investigations and treatment suggestions are offered for you to accept, change or add to.
  • Prescriptions are written with drug, dose, frequency and duration, and can be printed, saved as a PDF or shared.
  • On the wards there are doctor rounds, nurse rounds and a discharge summary. Nurse rounds are one part of Fullcurebank, not the whole of it.

Details are on the Fullcurebank page.

Pathbank: a clear reading of a lab report

Pathbank is an AI lab report interpreter. You upload a digital PDF, a scanned page or a phone photo, and Pathbank reads every parameter for the patient's age and sex and flags what is urgent.

  • Several reports can be compared side by side, with a chart of each parameter over time.
  • You can ask follow-up questions about a report in a chat.
  • Reports can be downloaded as a PDF or shared through a temporary read-only link that lasts 72 hours by default.
  • Doctors, labs, patients and caregivers each get their own view.

Pathbank here means the Lexmedix app at lexmedix.in/pathbank/. Details are on the Pathbank page.
