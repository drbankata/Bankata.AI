Why these products exist

A radiologist reads 50 to 80 studies a day. At ten minutes of typing each, documentation alone can fill a working day. The same pattern shows up everywhere he has worked: clinicians re-typing histories, labs explaining the same values, ward teams writing discharges late into the evening, and supervisors chasing attendance on paper.

Each Bankata product removes one of those burdens. They share a few rules: start from sensible defaults, change only what needs changing, check the work before it leaves, and keep the professional in charge.

The products are built in Noida, used on phones, and priced so that a single doctor can afford them.
