Radbank

Dictate only what is abnormal. Radbank writes the rest of the report.

Every template opens pre-filled with clinically accurate normal findings. You speak once, the AI updates only the sections your words touch, then checks the report before you sign it.

  • Report by exception. Structured templates for CT, MRI, ultrasound and X-ray open with normal defaults. Upload your own templates too.
  • One dictation, routed for you. Say all your findings in one go. The AI works out which sections they belong to and changes only those.
  • Automatic RADS scoring. BI-RADS, LI-RADS, Lung-RADS, TI-RADS, PI-RADS and O-RADS are scored from your findings and added to the impression.
  • Seven-module quality check. Seven independent AI checks look for gaps, clinical logic errors, guideline misses and risk flags before the report leaves your desk.

From ₹300 per user per month, with a ₹100 trial.

Fullcurebank

Consultations, ward rounds and discharges, written as you work.

A live note builds itself while you take the history and examine the patient. Fullcurebank suggests differentials, investigations and treatment for you to accept or change, then produces the prescription, discharge summary and patient pack.

  • Quick OPD. Dictate or type the encounter and get a structured consultation in minutes.
  • Live note. The right-hand panel turns what you enter into a clean clinical record as you go.
  • Differentials you control. Diagnosis suggestions from symptoms and findings. Accept, change or add your own.
  • Red-flag alerts. Warning signs are caught by a rules engine instantly, before any AI call.

From ₹300 per user per month, with a ₹100 trial.

Pathbank

Upload a lab report. Get a clear reading, the flags and the trend.

Photograph or upload any lab report. Pathbank reads it, interprets every value for the patient's age and sex, marks what is urgent and compares it with earlier reports.

  • Photo or PDF upload. Text is read from scanned pages and phone photos as well as digital PDFs.
  • Age- and sex-aware reading. The patient's age, sex and history are used to interpret every parameter.
  • Urgency flags. Values that need attention are marked so nothing important is missed.
  • Trends across reports. Compare several reports side by side with charts of each parameter over time.

From ₹300 per user per month, with a ₹100 trial.

Treatbank

A pile of old prescriptions and reports, turned into one clean patient history.

Upload prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries and radiology reports together. Treatbank extracts the text, even from scans and handwriting, and builds a structured record you can question in a clinical chat.

  • Upload everything at once. PDFs and images of prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries and radiology reports.
  • Reads scans and handwriting. Digital PDFs are read instantly; scanned and handwritten pages go through OCR.
  • A structured record. Patient details, chronological history, medications, investigations, diagnoses, summary and future plan.
  • Clinical chat. Ask questions about the compiled history and get answers grounded in it.

Questions people ask

What does the price include?

Radbank, Fullcurebank and Pathbank are sold as All-Inclusive AI: ₹300 per user per month, which is ₹100 platform access plus ₹200 of AI credits. There are no API keys to manage. Unused credits expire monthly and top-ups are available.

Can I try before I subscribe?

Radbank, Fullcurebank and Pathbank offer a ₹100 one-time trial with ₹50 of AI credits. For the other products, ask us for a demo.

Do the products work on a phone?

Yes. Every product is designed for phones first and also works on tablets and desktops.

Where is my data kept?

Each product keeps every organisation's data separate inside its own secure database. Ask us if you need details for a security review.

Does AI make the final decision?

No. The products draft, organise and check. The professional reviews and decides.

Can my team or hospital buy together?

Yes. Team plans cover 2 to 20 users and hospital plans cover 21 users and more.
