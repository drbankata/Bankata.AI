What is Lexmedix? Our eight work tools

Lexmedix is a doctor-led team that builds eight products: six AI apps and two for NABL and NABH work. See what each one does and what it costs.

Lexmedix is a doctor-led team that builds eight products for healthcare, legal and facility teams. Six are AI apps: Radbank, Fullcurebank, Pathbank, Treatbank, Legalbank and Facility Management SaaS. Each turns what you say or upload into finished work that a person then checks. Two are for NABL and NABH work: Dxbank, an LIS and RIS for laboratories and imaging centres, and Clinibank, a clinic system for stand-alone clinics and centres. Dr Sudhanshu Bankata founded Lexmedix and leads the team.

Frequently asked questions

What is Lexmedix?

Lexmedix is a family of eight work tools for doctors, radiologists, labs, imaging centres, clinics, legal teams and housekeeping companies. Our team designs and builds them, and Dr Sudhanshu Bankata leads the work. The six AI apps work on a phone first and also run on tablets and desktops. Dxbank and Clinibank run on your own Windows computer. The home page is lexmedix.in.

What does each Lexmedix product do?

Radbank lets a radiologist dictate only what is abnormal, and it writes the rest of the report. Fullcurebank writes consultations, ward rounds and discharges as the doctor works. Pathbank reads an uploaded lab report and shows the flags and the trend. Treatbank turns a pile of old prescriptions and reports into one clean patient history. Legalbank keeps cases, documents and arguments in one AI workspace for a legal team. Facility Management SaaS handles attendance, payroll, billing and cleaning quality for housekeeping companies. Dxbank is an LIS and RIS for laboratories and imaging centres with an NABL and NABH evidence dashboard. Clinibank is a clinic system with an NABH Assessment page for clinics.

Which Lexmedix product is for radiologists?

Radbank by Lexmedix is the reporting tool for radiologists and other diagnostic specialists. Its structured templates open with normal findings in every section, so you dictate only what is abnormal. The AI drafts and organises, and the radiologist still reviews and signs the report. See Radbank.

Which Lexmedix product helps a lab or imaging centre prepare for NABL or NABH?

Dxbank by Lexmedix. It is an LIS and RIS that runs registration, billing, results, radiology booking, safety and consent, and reports. Its Assessment page lists the clause codes of NABH medical imaging, NABL laboratory and NABL radiology, shows the register that answers each one and how many of your records sit in it, runs named gap checks, and makes an evidence pack for the assessor. See Dxbank.

Which Lexmedix product helps a clinic prepare for NABH?

Clinibank by Lexmedix. It is a clinic system for stand-alone clinics and centres, with modules for many kinds of centre. Its Assessment page covers the NABH standards for clinics, runs clinic gap checks, and makes an evidence pack. It also has a self-check against the NABH digital health standard for clinic software. See Clinibank.

Does Dxbank or Clinibank make a lab or clinic NABL or NABH compliant?

No software can. Both show your own records against the clause codes and list the gaps. Neither gives a score or says you comply. The assessor decides.

Is Fullcurebank a nursing tool?

No. Fullcurebank by Lexmedix is built mainly for doctors: consultations, ward rounds and discharges are written as the doctor works. Nurse rounds are one part of it, alongside doctor rounds and the discharge summary. See Fullcurebank.

What is Pathbank by Lexmedix?

Pathbank by Lexmedix is an AI lab report interpreter. You upload a PDF or a phone photo of a lab report, and it reads every value for the patient's age and sex, marks what is urgent and compares the report with earlier ones. It lives at lexmedix.in/pathbank/.

Is Legalbank a medical-legal tool?

No. Legalbank by Lexmedix is for lawyers and legal teams. It keeps cases, documents, arguments and hearings together, and helps with document analysis, argument building and drafting. See Legalbank.

Does Facility Management SaaS run healthcare centres?

No. Facility Management SaaS by Lexmedix is for housekeeping and facility companies. It covers attendance, payroll for India (PF and ESI), client invoices and cleaning quality checks, and supervisors can record attendance for workers who have no smartphone. See Facility Management SaaS.

How much do Lexmedix products cost?

Radbank, Fullcurebank and Pathbank are priced at Rs 300 per user per month on the All-Inclusive AI plan, which includes the AI. Pricing for Treatbank, Legalbank and Facility Management SaaS is on request, sized to your team. Dxbank and Clinibank are licensed by the year for each lab or centre, not by user, with no monthly cloud charge, and the price is on request. See the pricing page.

Is Lexmedix related to other companies with similar names?

No. Lexmedix (lexmedix.in) is not related to other businesses with similar names, including pharmaceutical and law-related look-alikes. Our products are Radbank, Fullcurebank, Pathbank, Treatbank, Legalbank, Facility Management SaaS, Dxbank and Clinibank, and our address is lexmedix.in.

Who is behind Lexmedix?

Lexmedix was founded by Dr Sudhanshu Bankata, a radiologist and doctor in healthcare since 1995, who is also an NABL assessor for radiology. Our team builds and supports the products under his lead. You can read more on the About page.

Does the AI replace the doctor?

No. The AI drafts and organises. The doctor still reviews and decides. Dxbank and Clinibank use no AI.
