What Fullcurebank does

Quick OPD

Dictate or type the encounter and get a structured consultation in minutes.

Live note

The right-hand panel turns what you enter into a clean clinical record as you go.

Differentials you control

Diagnosis suggestions from symptoms and findings. Accept, change or add your own.

Red-flag alerts

Warning signs are caught by a rules engine instantly, before any AI call.

Prescriptions

Choose drugs, dose, frequency and duration. The prescription is formatted for print, PDF or sharing.

Doctor rounds

Ward-round entries, clinical notes and orders for admitted patients.

Nurse rounds

Shift notes by voice, medication record, fluid balance, care bundles, handoffs and family updates.

Questions about Fullcurebank

Is Fullcurebank only for hospitals?

No. Single doctors use Quick OPD and prescriptions; hospitals add rounds, discharge and departmental modules.

Can patients use it?

Yes. Patients can register for their own portal to see the documents their care team shares.

Who makes the final clinical decision?

You do. Every suggestion is shown for review and can be accepted, edited or rejected.

Does it work on Indian mobile networks?

Yes. It is built mobile-first and routes data so that it works on Indian internet providers.
