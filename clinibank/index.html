Lexmedix / Clinibank

Clinic system for stand-alone clinics and centres

A clinic system that shows your records against the NABH clinic standards and lists the gaps.

Clinibank by Lexmedix is a clinic system for stand-alone clinics and centres: appointments, consultation, prescriptions, billing and registers, with an Assessment page that shows which of your records answer the NABH clinic standards and where the gaps are. Clinibank runs on your own Windows computer and needs no internet. It handles appointments, consultation, prescriptions, billing, lab and imaging, and the registers a clinic must keep. Its Assessment page shows which of your records answer each NABH clause for your kind of centre, and one button makes an evidence pack for the assessor.

What Clinibank does

Everything in Dxbank

Registration, billing, lab and radiology, reports by print, PDF and WhatsApp, backups and MIS. Clinibank is built on Dxbank, so the NABL and NABH imaging and laboratory evidence tools are all there.

The clinic day

Appointments and a queue, consultation with specialty forms, prescriptions, procedures and treatment plans, packages and sessions, day care, a pharmacy or optical store, and recall.

Eighteen kinds of centre

Dental, eye, skin and aesthetic, IVF and ART, oncology day care, dialysis, diabetes, paediatric, obstetrics, physiotherapy, ENT, cardiology, occupational health, Ayurveda and homeopathy, psychiatry, endoscopy, weight loss and general clinics. Each is a module, so you pay only for yours.

NABH Assessment page for clinics

Pick the standard for your centre: Allopathic Clinics 2nd edition, Small Healthcare Organisations 3rd edition, Dental, Entry Level Dental, Eye Care, ART Clinics, Ayush Treatment and Wellness, or Entry Level Ayush. Every clause code gets a short label, its level (core, commitment, achievement or excellence), the register that answers it and a live count.

Digital health self-check

A page built on the NABH Digital Health Standards for Clinic Management Systems. Every element shows its status, the screen that answers it and a note, and it says plainly which need the network and which Clinibank does not do.

Fourteen clinic gap checks

Consultation notes left unsigned, a procedure with no consent or time-out, allergy not asked, a high-risk medicine not double-checked, expired stock, a scope not disinfected, a day with no biomedical waste entry. Each shows a count and the rows.

Clinic indicators and registers

The ten indicators of the Allopathic Clinics standard, with a quarterly sheet to print or export. Registers for biomedical waste, needle-stick injury, duty and attendance, and the statutory ones: Schedule H1 and X, NDPS, PCPNDT Form F, ART and mental healthcare records with restricted access.

Evidence pack and sampling

A random pick with a seed that repeats it, and a zip of your registers, the indicator sheet and the gap list. Restricted records such as ART are added only by a user with the right who ticks the box, and that is audited.

Internal quality control and non-conformity follow-up

The same Westgard-rule charts and the same rule as Dxbank: an incident or non-conformity cannot close without the correction, the corrective action and the effectiveness check.

Questions about Clinibank

Does it need the internet?

No. Clinibank runs on your own Windows computer and keeps your data there. Internet is needed only to send a report by WhatsApp or mail. It has no AI.

Does it make me NABH compliant?

No software can. Clinibank shows your records against the clause codes, lists the gaps and makes an evidence pack. It gives no score and never says you comply. The assessor judges.

Which centres is it for?

Stand-alone clinics and centres, from a single-doctor clinic to a polyclinic, in eighteen kinds of centre. It is not a hospital system.

Are the starter forms and ranges checked for my practice?

They are starting points. Your own doctor must confirm reference ranges, forms, doses and drug lists before use.

Can I try it first?

Yes. There is a demo for 30 days or 100 visits. Reports printed in the demo are marked as not for clinical use. Ask us to book it.

What does it cost?

Clinibank is priced competitively. It is licensed by the year for each centre, not by user, with a module for your specialty and options for lab, imaging, store and day care. Price on request.

What happens when the licence ends?

You get a reminder first. After the grace period Clinibank becomes read-only, never locked, so you can still open and print old records.

Security and privacy

  • Your data stays on your own computer. The standard Clinibank makes no network calls and has no AI.
  • Roles decide who can see a note, a prescription or the money.
  • Restricted records such as ART and mental healthcare are visible only to a user with the right, and every look is audited.
  • Every change to clinical or billing data goes into an audit trail that shows tampering.
  • Backups run every day and are encrypted.
