What Facility Management does

Attendance

Bulk and voice entry, with unusual patterns flagged automatically.

Payroll for India

PF, ESI, overtime and deductions calculated for you.

Billing and invoices

Client invoices from attendance, with penalties tied to task quality.

Sites and health scores

Staff assignments and a health score for every site.

Task engine

SOP-based tasks with photo checks and AI quality scores.

AI supervisor

Spots staff shortages and missed tasks across sites.

Voice in Hindi and Hinglish

Supervisors give commands and log incidents by speaking.

No-smartphone mode

Workers without smartphones can still take part.

Inventory

Material tracking, wastage and reorder alerts.

Dashboards

An owner dashboard with KPIs and AI insights, and a read-only client dashboard.

Questions about Facility Management

Do workers need smartphones?

No. Supervisors can record attendance and tasks for them, and there is a no-smartphone mode.

Does payroll follow Indian rules?

Yes. PF, ESI and overtime are calculated following Indian rules.

Can my clients see reports?

Yes. Clients get a read-only dashboard of attendance, invoices and reports.

Is the app in Hindi?

The sign-in and voice features work in Hindi and Hinglish as well as English.
