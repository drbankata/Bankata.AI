What Radbank does

Report by exception

Structured templates for CT, MRI, ultrasound and X-ray open with normal defaults. Upload your own templates too.

One dictation, routed for you

Say all your findings in one go. The AI works out which sections they belong to and changes only those.

Automatic RADS scoring

BI-RADS, LI-RADS, Lung-RADS, TI-RADS, PI-RADS and O-RADS are scored from your findings and added to the impression.

Seven-module quality check

Seven independent AI checks look for gaps, clinical logic errors, guideline misses and risk flags before the report leaves your desk.

AI second opinion

One click gives a structured second read of your report. Compare, edit and finalise in the same screen.

Critical finding alerts

Critical findings flag the study STAT on the worklist and prepare an email and a WhatsApp message for the referring team.

Patient pack

A patient-friendly summary of the report in the patient's own language.

Fetal medicine module

FMF-compliant NT, nasal bone and tricuspid screening, risk algorithms and INTERGROWTH-21st biometry charts. A paid add-on.

Six more specialties

Templates for pathology, cardiology, endoscopy, pulmonology, ophthalmology and nuclear medicine reports.

Runs your practice

Worklist, check-in and appointments, GST billing with UPI QR, NABH turnaround tracking, referrer and patient portals, practice analytics.

ResearchBank

Turn reports into papers: PubMed-linked literature search, built-in statistical tests, EQUATOR checklists, export to DOCX or LaTeX.

Referrer outreach and CME

Awareness content, referrer updates, scan-slot and CME calendars, conference-ready case decks.

Questions about Radbank

Which specialties does Radbank cover?

Radiology (CT, MRI, ultrasound, X-ray) plus pathology, cardiology, endoscopy, pulmonology, ophthalmology and nuclear medicine templates.

Can I use my own report templates?

Yes. You can upload your existing custom templates alongside the built-in ones.

Does the AI replace the radiologist?

No. Radbank drafts, routes and checks. You review and sign every report.

How do I pay?

Subscriptions run through Razorpay, which accepts UPI apps and domestic and international cards.
