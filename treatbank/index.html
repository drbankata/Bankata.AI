What Treatbank does

Upload everything at once

PDFs and images of prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries and radiology reports.

Reads scans and handwriting

Digital PDFs are read instantly; scanned and handwritten pages go through OCR.

A structured record

Patient details, chronological history, medications, investigations, diagnoses, summary and future plan.

Clinical chat

Ask questions about the compiled history and get answers grounded in it.

Questions about Treatbank

What documents work?

Prescriptions, lab reports, discharge summaries and radiology reports, as PDFs or images.

Does it read handwriting?

Yes, handwritten and scanned pages go through OCR. Very poor scans may need a check.

Can I keep the result?

Yes. Reports are saved to your history and can be exported as PDF.
