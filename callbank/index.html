Lexmedix / Callbank

An AI voice call desk for labs, clinics and hospitals

An AI voice call desk that answers your phone in Hindi, English and Bangla, and is linked to your own records.

Callbank by Lexmedix is an AI voice call desk for labs, clinics and hospitals: it answers your phone in Hindi, English and Bangla, takes appointment, callback and complaint requests for your front desk, and is linked to your own records. Callbank answers your published number on a dedicated line. It gives the facts you have approved, finds the right doctor or department, takes appointment, callback and complaint requests for your front desk, and tells a caller whether a report is ready after checking who is asking. Linked to Dxbank or Clinibank it reads only the minimum it needs from your own program; without a link it works from your approved answers and the lists you give us.

What Callbank does

Answers in the caller's language

Hindi, English and Bangla. The agent follows the language the caller speaks and says your facility name the way each language says it.

Facts you approved

We build the answers from your price list, timings and policies. You approve every answer on your own screen before the call desk may say it, and can correct a fact or add one at any time. You can download all your answers.

Finds the doctor or department

Doctor, department, day and language, from your linked program or your lists. It never promises a time it was not shown.

Requests for your front desk

Appointment, reschedule, cancel, callback and complaint requests land in a Call Desk screen with the caller's words. A linked program shows them for staff to accept, so nothing changes in your records without your people.

Appointments offered from open slots

From the slots your program shares, the agent offers up to three open ones, records the choice, and says only that a request is made until your staff confirm.

Report status with an identity check

A caller is asked for a second detail, a wrong answer is counted, and repeated wrong answers stop the call from saying more. Every disclosure is written to an audit trail. The agent never reads out a result.

Emergencies

Any suspected emergency gets one instruction: hang up and call your emergency number. The agent gives no medical advice and does not diagnose.

Complaints with a deadline

Each complaint gets the deadline you set and is flagged to the owner when it passes.

Insights for your manager

A tab in your Call Desk shows calls by day and by hour, how calls ended, how quickly requests are confirmed, the questions the agent could not answer, and report-status answers given without staff time. Print it, or download counts-only files with no phone numbers and no words from any call. Where there are too few calls to read a trend, it says so instead of guessing.

Reminders and feedback

Appointment reminders, report-ready calls and feedback calls are planned as lists for staff to run, inside calling hours you confirm.

Calls on record, kept safe

A recording notice you approve, transcripts stored with long numbers removed, a retention period you set, and export and deletion on request.

Call minutes you control

Prepaid call minutes with a balance, alerts as they run low, and a top-up from your own screen. At an empty balance you choose whether callers hear that the desk is unavailable or are still answered.

Tested before every change

Every new version of the agent must pass a written test suite, including emergency, identity-check and no-advice cases, before we use it for clients.

How it works

  1. We set upWe create your account, your dedicated number and your onboarding checklist. We collect the documents the telephone line needs.
  2. We build, you approveWe draft your answers from your documents. You read them on your screen and approve.
  3. Link your programIf you use Dxbank or Clinibank, we issue a code that switches the link on. Only the minimum the agent needs is sent, and never results, diagnoses or bills.
  4. Go liveYou forward your published number to your Callbank number, always, after hours or when nobody answers. We test it with you first.

Questions about Callbank

Does it give medical advice?

No. It routes callers to a department and tells anyone with a possible emergency to hang up and call your emergency number.

Can it book an appointment by itself?

It records a request and offers open slots from your program. Your staff confirm. It never tells a caller an appointment is booked before that.

Will it read out test results?

No. For a report it says only ready, not ready or where to collect it, and only after an identity check.

What does it need from me?

Your price list, timings, doctor list, test list and policies, the documents for the telephone line, and your approval of the answers. We give you a printable list of exactly what is still outstanding.

Do I have to use Dxbank or Clinibank?

No. Without a link it works from your approved answers and the lists you load into the Call Desk. With a link it can also tell callers about doctors, slots and report status.

Whose data is it?

Yours. You are responsible for it and we process it for you under an agreement we sign with you. You can export or delete it.

What does it cost?

Pricing is on request. It is a monthly fee for the call desk, an extra monthly fee for a linked program, prepaid call minutes, and the telephone number.
