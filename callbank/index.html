Lexmedix / Callbank

AI voice call desk and front-desk CRM

An AI voice call desk for any clinic, lab or hospital, of any size.

Callbank by Lexmedix is an AI voice call desk for any clinic, practice, lab, radiology centre or hospital, of any size: it answers your phone in Hindi, English and any regional language of your choice, takes appointment, callback and complaint requests for your front desk, works without any of our software, and is integrated with Hospibank, Clinibank and Dxbank. It answers your published number on a dedicated line, gives the facts you have approved, finds the right doctor or department, and tells a caller whether a report is ready after checking who is asking. When linked to your program it reads only the minimum it needs. For a hospital it becomes a front-desk CRM, with every caller's request, complaint and callback on record.

What Callbank does

Needs none of our software

Callbank works for any clinic, practice, lab, radiology centre or hospital, whatever software you use or none. Nothing is installed at your place: your staff open the Call Desk in a browser, and the agent answers from your approved answers and the doctor, department and report lists you load.

A front-desk CRM for hospitals

Every caller's request, complaint and callback is on record with the caller's words and the time, and each callback carries its topic. Your departments, OPD doctors, consultation fees, health-check packages and department hours are in one place, and complaints carry the deadline you set.

Integrated with Hospibank

Linked to Hospibank, the agent knows your OPD doctors and their departments, open slots, fees, packages, department hours and staff lines, and can tell an outpatient whether a lab or radiology report is ready. Appointment requests become real OPD bookings once your staff confirm, and complaints and callbacks go into the hospital's quality register. Nothing about an admission, a result, a diagnosis or a bill is ever sent.

Integrated with Clinibank and Dxbank

Linked to Clinibank, it offers open slots and your staff confirm them into the clinic's appointment book, and attended and missed visits drive reminders and recall. Linked to Dxbank, it gives live report status, and a report held for payment is never called ready.

Answers in the caller's language

Hindi, English and any regional language of your choice. The agent follows the language the caller speaks and says your facility name the way each language says it.

Sends the caller to the right department

A caller who describes a problem is pointed to the department that sees it, from your own list. It routes; it does not diagnose.

Facts you approved

We build the answers from your price list, timings and policies. You approve every answer on your own screen before the call desk may say it, and can correct a fact or add one at any time. You can download all your answers.

Finds the doctor or department

Doctor, department, day and language, from your linked program or your lists. It never promises a time it was not shown.

Requests for your front desk

Appointment, reschedule, cancel, callback and complaint requests land in a Call Desk screen with the caller's words. A linked program shows them for staff to accept, so nothing changes in your records without your people.

Appointments offered from open slots

From the slots your program shares, the agent offers up to three open ones, records the choice, and says only that a request is made until your staff confirm. Once they confirm or move it, a message to the caller is ready to send by WhatsApp in one click.

Report status with an identity check

A caller is asked for a second detail, a wrong answer is counted, and repeated wrong answers stop the call from saying more. Every disclosure is written to an audit trail. The agent never reads out a result.

Emergencies

Any suspected emergency gets one instruction: hang up and call your emergency number. The agent gives no medical advice and does not diagnose.

Complaints with a deadline

Each complaint gets the deadline you set, is marked escalated in the Call Desk when it passes, and can be closed only with a reason.

Insights for your manager

A tab in your Call Desk shows calls by day and by hour, how calls ended, how quickly requests are confirmed, the questions the agent could not answer, and report-status answers given without staff time. Print it, or download counts-only files with no phone numbers and no words from any call. Where there are too few calls to read a trend, it says so instead of guessing.

Reminders and feedback

Appointment reminders, report-ready calls and feedback calls are planned as lists for staff to run, inside calling hours you confirm.

Calls on record, kept safe

A recording notice you approve, transcripts stored with long numbers removed, a retention period you set, and export and deletion on request.

Call minutes you control

Prepaid call minutes with a balance, a warning on your screen as they run low, and a top-up from your own screen. At an empty balance you choose whether callers hear that the desk is unavailable or are still answered.

Tested before every change

Every new version of the agent must pass a written test suite, including emergency, identity-check and no-advice cases, before we use it for clients.

Questions about Callbank

Does it give medical advice?

No. It routes callers to a department and tells anyone with a possible emergency to hang up and call your emergency number.

Can it book an appointment by itself?

It records a request and offers open slots from your program. Your staff confirm. It never tells a caller an appointment is booked before that.

Will it read out test results?

No. For a report it says only ready, not ready or where to collect it, and only after an identity check.

What does it need from me?

Your price list, timings, doctor list, test list and policies, the documents for the telephone line, and your approval of the answers. We give you a printable list of exactly what is still outstanding.

Do I have to use Hospibank, Clinibank or Dxbank?

No. Callbank needs none of our software. Without a link it works from your approved answers and the doctor, department and report lists you load into the Call Desk, so it can still find a doctor and give report status. A link adds open slots, bookings confirmed into your program and live report status.

Is it only for small clinics?

No. It suits a solo practice and a large hospital alike. A hospital loads its departments, OPD doctors, packages and hours, and with Hospibank linked, appointment requests become OPD bookings and complaints go into the quality register.

Whose data is it?

Yours. You are responsible for it and we process it for you under an agreement we sign with you. You can export or delete it.

What does it cost?

Pricing is on request. It is a monthly fee for the call desk, an extra monthly fee for a linked program, prepaid call minutes, and the telephone number.
