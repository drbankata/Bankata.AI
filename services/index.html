Questions about the services

Who do you work with?

Hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centres and chains, doctors planning a new facility, and healthcare ventures looking for investors. Websites are for doctors, practices and small businesses.

Do I need to use Bankata software to hire you?

No. The advice stands on its own. Where one of the products fits your problem, we will say so, but you are never required to use it.

How is consulting priced?

Each engagement is quoted after a first conversation, because scope differs a great deal between, say, a website and a hospital planning project. Nothing is priced on this page.

Do you provide teleradiology for clients outside India?

Yes. Teleradiology is provided through Dr Bankata's own firm, RadBank Imaging Solutions, for both Indian and international clients. Tell us your modalities and volumes and we will discuss how it would work.

Are your cost estimates exact?

No. Equipment and project costs are planning-grade estimates to help you decide and budget. They must be verified with vendors and your own advisers before you commit.

Will you raise the money for me?

We prepare the plan, the numbers and the pitch, and help structure the deal. We cannot promise that funding will be raised, and this is not investment, legal or tax advice.

Where are you based?

In India, working with clients across the country and, for teleradiology, abroad. Much of the work can be done remotely.

Tell us what you are planning

A new centre, an accreditation, a funding round, a hiring need or a website. Choose the service and describe it in a line or two. We reply personally.
