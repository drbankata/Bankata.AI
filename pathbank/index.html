What Pathbank does

Photo or PDF upload

Text is read from scanned pages and phone photos as well as digital PDFs.

Age- and sex-aware reading

The patient's age, sex and history are used to interpret every parameter.

Urgency flags

Values that need attention are marked so nothing important is missed.

Trends across reports

Compare several reports side by side with charts of each parameter over time.

Ask about the report

Follow-up questions in a chat about that specific report.

Share and download

Read-only share links that expire after 72 hours, and PDF downloads.

Roles for everyone

Separate views for doctors, labs, patients and caregivers.

Quality tools for labs

NABL-oriented quality dashboards for laboratories.

Questions about Pathbank

Which reports can I upload?

Standard laboratory reports as PDFs or photos, including scanned pages.

Is a doctor still needed?

Yes. Pathbank explains results and flags concerns; treatment decisions stay with a doctor.

Can I share a report without giving login access?

Yes. Share links are read-only and expire after 72 hours.
