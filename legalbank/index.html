What Legalbank does

Cases and matters

Intake, issues, documents, hearings and a calendar for every matter.

Document analysis

Ask questions of documents, compare versions, run OCR and translate.

Argument builder

Structured arguments and counter-arguments for each issue.

Precedents and citations

Find and check precedents and citations for your research.

Drafting

Notices, contracts and templates drafted section by section.

Judge's bench

Cause list, matter briefs, orders and analytics for judges.

Mediation

Pre-briefing, BATNA analysis, impasse diagnosis and settlement agreements.

Corporate and notary

Contract and NDA review, compliance, resolutions, stamp duty and notary registers.
